# Hey, I'm Darius 

I'm a Belgian marine biologist currently finishing my Master of Marine Biology at James Cook University (Australia).
Before that, I completed a Bachelor of Science at the Maastricht Science Programme (Maastricht University, Netherlands), where I focused on ecology and evolutionary biology.

## About me

As you can probably guess, I love nature. My interests are mostly towards the big things: marine megafauna, including cetaceans, seals, rays, sharks, and many others.
After a few years at university, I also realised that I was quite interested in the data analysis behind studying these animals and their ecosystems, particularly when it comes to population dynamics and modelling.

## Current research

I am currently conducting research for my master's thesis on biphasic recovery of coral reefs across the Great Barrier Reef.
This involves a variety of statistical and multivariate analyses, including GLMMs, PERMANOVA, SIMPER, data visualisation and exploration, ecological and statistical modelling
